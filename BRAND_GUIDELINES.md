# ProofWarden Brand Guidelines

## Brand Idea

ProofWarden is evidence infrastructure for AI-agent actions. The brand should feel dark, technical, protective, and enterprise-grade: a product that preserves proof without overclaiming judgment.

## Logo

Primary logo:

- Use `public/brand/proofwarden-logo.svg` for the full horizontal lockup.
- Use `public/brand/proofwarden-mark.svg` where space is constrained.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the shield height.
- Do not place the mark on busy imagery without a dark overlay.

## Color Palette

- Deep background: `#020617`
- Panel navy: `#03101F`
- White text: `#F8FAFC`
- Muted text: `#94A3B8`
- Proof cyan: `#22D3EE`
- Electric cyan: `#67E8F9`
- Trust blue: `#0EA5E9`
- Warden violet: `#8B5CF6`

## Wordmark

- Write the product name as `ProofWarden`.
- In the primary wordmark, `Proof` is white and `Warden` is cyan.
- Avoid all-caps body references except compact navigation or metadata labels.

## Visual Style

- Use dark, spacious layouts with restrained cyan and violet glow.
- Prefer thin borders, subtle grids, and proof-chain diagrams over decorative blobs.
- Keep UI copy direct, credible, and evidence-focused.
- Preserve the distinction between customer evidence vault, DUAL proof layer, and reviewer evidence record.

## Product Claims

ProofWarden does not determine compliance, assign liability, approve workflows, replace auditors, make legal decisions, or store raw evidence on DUAL.
